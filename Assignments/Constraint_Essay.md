# AgriTech

### Chi Thanh Tran, Luke Grochocki, Hebron Mekuria, Kian Palmer

Our group have not exactly done with creating a concrete plan on what we want to do, but the overall topic of the project will be around microelectronic system that help farmer in agricultural context, through either smart and power-efficient communication, or through cheap and easily available disease detection in plant.

## Economic

The main theme of the project will be a focus on power-efficiency, as well as budget-efficiency. This mean economic-wise, the project aim to minimize any financial (around 500$ at very most) and availability limitation, prioritizing cheap and easy-to-access modules and hardware. The main purpose of this choice is to create a solution that is more available to farmers, our main client, so that they can have access to said solution easily, without worrying about the price, or needing access to lab-graded equipment.

## Professional

While we believe this is a sound choice to create a solution both accessible to the client and to us for testing, this do directly affect the Professionalism of the project. Limiting ourselves to only commercial, low-cost equipment and electronic mean actively removing choices that can greatly increase the effectiveness and impact of our product. This also mean that a lot of our choice in more specialized solution will not be reachable. An example would be an unavailability in multispectral camera or image processing, which is a very powerful tool in plant disease detection that can further elevate our expertise, but would not fit our vision of a low-budget solution.

## Environmental

Environmental limitation was also something we have to consider heavily going into the project. Disease detection, at its core, require a system that is sensitive to the smallest of change in environment data, and as such, a very difficult problem to be solvable with just one general solution. To make sure the scope of the project stay viable, we limit our own goal to only a specific plant, a specific disease (or two at most), and a specific area of the Ohio region. This is a needed limitation to avoid wasting time and money trying to reach unreachable expectation.
